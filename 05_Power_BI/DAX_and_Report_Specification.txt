POWER BI DAX MEASURES
======================

Create these with Modeling > New measure. If you already created a measure, do not create it twice.

BATTTING (table: batting_summary)
Total Runs = SUM(batting_summary[runs])
Total Batters = DISTINCTCOUNT(batting_summary[batter])
Average Strike Rate = AVERAGE(batting_summary[strike_rate])
Total Fours = SUM(batting_summary[fours])
Total Sixes = SUM(batting_summary[sixes])
Boundary Runs = [Total Fours] * 4 + [Total Sixes] * 6

BOWLING (table: bowling_summary)
Total Wickets = SUM(bowling_summary[wickets])
Total Bowlers = DISTINCTCOUNT(bowling_summary[bowler])
Total Runs Conceded = SUM(bowling_summary[runs_conceded])
Total Legal Balls = SUM(bowling_summary[legal_balls])
Overall Economy = DIVIDE([Total Runs Conceded] * 6, [Total Legal Balls], 0)

Format Average Strike Rate and Overall Economy as Decimal number, 2 decimal places.

REPORT PAGE 1: Overview
- Four Card visuals: Total Runs, Total Wickets, Total Batters, Total Bowlers
- Clustered bar chart: Y-axis batter, X-axis runs; visual-level batter filter Top N = 10, By value runs
- Clustered bar chart: Y-axis bowler, X-axis wickets; visual-level bowler filter Top N = 10, By value wickets

REPORT PAGE 2: Batting Analysis
- Cards: Total Runs, Average Strike Rate, Total Fours, Total Sixes
- Bar chart: batter by runs, Top N = 10
- Bar chart: batter by strike_rate (interpret alongside balls_faced)
- Table: batter, runs, balls_faced, fours, sixes, strike_rate

REPORT PAGE 3: Bowling Analysis
- Cards: Total Wickets, Total Runs Conceded, Overall Economy, Total Bowlers
- Bar chart: bowler by wickets, Top N = 10
- Bar chart: bowler by economy; consider filtering legal_balls >= 60
- Table: bowler, matches, wickets, runs_conceded, legal_balls, economy, bowling_average

MODEL
Keep batting_summary and bowling_summary as separate tables. Do not merge or append these summary-level tables. No relationship is required for these visuals.

DATA QUALITY
The supplied files contain 223 batter records and 451 bowler records. Validate source scope before describing totals as official Men's T20 World Cup 2024 statistics.
